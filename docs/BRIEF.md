# Ethernet 0/1–10V dimmer

ESP32 with Ethernet. 16 channels of 0/1–10V dimming, and 16 channels of inline power monitoring for connected AC loads. 24×7 operation. Low parasitic power drain important.. 24VDC power supply. Budget is $10/channel excluding case and power supply. Nice-to-have: two Ethernet ports to allow connection to different networks for redundancy. Nice-to-have: ability to have two units operate as a redundant pair with automatic failover.
